# V11 — evidence-led comparisons

A run is eligible when it has valid times and distance >=2km and user-provided RPE (1–10) and pain (0–3). Pairs require >=7 and <=42 days separation, distance difference <=10%, and difference in RPE <=1. The most recent valid pair is selected and pace and optional mean HR are reported; no improvement is inferred with missing subjective measurements.

This is descriptive only. Matching distances and subjective effort cannot control for weather, route elevation, temperature, sensor errors or physiology, so a faster pace is not proof of adaptation. Comparisons are not a training intervention or approval for faster workouts.
